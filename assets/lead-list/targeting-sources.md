# Targeting Sources — Where to Build the Lead List

Concrete places to source the 40 week-1 leads. All public / standard-practice. Goal: 15 partners (Tier 1), 15 signal companies (Tier 2), 10 stretch (Tier 3).

---

## Tier 1 — NetSuite partner firms (15)

| Source | How to use |
|--------|-----------|
| **NetSuite Partner Directory** (netsuite.com/portal/partners) | Filter by region/specialization. Target Silver/Authorized + boutiques, not Platinum giants. |
| **ERP Research partner directory** (600+ partners) | Browse smaller/regional firms; grab founder names. |
| **LinkedIn search** | `"NetSuite Solution Provider"`, `"NetSuite Partner"`, `"NetSuite Practice Lead"`, `"NetSuite Director"` — filter to small firms (1–50 employees). |
| **NetSuite/SuiteWorld LinkedIn groups** | Active partner principals post here. |
| **BPO partner lists** | Firms running clients' accounting on NetSuite — prime for the margin pitch. |

Capture: firm, principal/lead name, title, LinkedIn, partner tier (if visible), why-now signal.

## Tier 2 — Companies hiring a NetSuite admin (15) ← highest intent

| Source | How to use |
|--------|-----------|
| **Indeed / LinkedIn Jobs** | Search `"NetSuite Administrator"`, `"NetSuite Developer"`, `"Business Systems Analyst NetSuite"`, `"ERP Analyst NetSuite"`. Each open req = a company with active NetSuite pain + budget. |
| **Company → decision-maker** | For each hiring company, find the CFO/Controller/VP Ops/IT Director on LinkedIn (the hiring manager or their boss). |
| **Glassdoor / company careers pages** | Cross-check active NetSuite roles. |

Capture: company, the finance/IT leader (not just the recruiter), the job-post signal + date.

## Tier 3 — NetSuite mid-market, no active signal (10)

| Source | How to use |
|--------|-----------|
| **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** | Filter: company size 50–1,000, title (CFO/Controller/VP Ops/IT Dir), keyword "NetSuite" in profile/company. Best tool once you're prospecting (~$99/mo, clear ROI). |
| **BuiltWith / tech-stack tools** | Find companies running NetSuite + a CRM/3PL/bank feed (integration surface = your pitch). |
| **Industry associations** | Distribution, manufacturing, e-commerce groups — heavy NetSuite usage. |
| **r/Netsuite + NetSuite Professionals groups** | People posting problems = warm-ish, but engage genuinely, don't pitch in-thread. |

## Enrichment checklist (per lead)
- [ ] Decision-maker identified (not a gatekeeper)
- [ ] LinkedIn URL
- [ ] Email if findable (Hunter/Apollo free tiers, or company pattern) — optional early
- [ ] The **specific signal** ("hiring NS admin 6/20," "posted re: automation," "went live Q4")
- [ ] Channel chosen (LinkedIn DM vs. email)
- [ ] Added to `lead-list-template.csv` with status "Not Contacted"

## Quality over quantity
40 *well-researched, signal-backed, decision-maker* leads beat 400 scraped names. Each should have a real "why now" you can open the message with. If you can't name the signal, it's a Tier-3/nurture, not an outreach target.

## Tooling (defer spend until prospecting starts)
- **Free first:** manual LinkedIn + Indeed + company sites.
- **When volume justifies (clear ROI):** Sales Navigator (~$99/mo) for sourcing; Apollo/Hunter free tiers for emails.
- **Later:** a light CRM (or keep using the CSV) once you have 50+ active leads.
