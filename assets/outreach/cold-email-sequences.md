# Cold Email Sequences — End-Buyers

For company-side prospects (CFO/Controller/VP Ops/IT Director at NetSuite mid-market). Best paired with a buying **signal** (hiring a NetSuite admin, recent go-live, automation post). Goal of the sequence = a 20-min fit call.

> Principles: short, signal-led, *their* problem first, one clear ask, easy out. No "I hope this finds you well." No résumé dump. 4–5 touches over ~2–3 weeks, then stop.

---

## SEQUENCE A — Signal: company is hiring a NetSuite Admin

### Email 1 — the signal hook
**Subject:** `your NetSuite admin search`
> Hi [First name],
>
> Saw [Company] is hiring a NetSuite administrator — usually a sign the system's doing more than the current team can keep up with manually.
>
> I'm an AI systems architect who specializes in NetSuite. Before you add headcount, it's often worth knowing which of those manual workflows (close, AP, reconciliation, reporting, integrations) AI can take off the team's plate entirely — sometimes reducing *how much* admin you need to hire.
>
> I run a 2-week NetSuite AI Leverage Audit that maps exactly that, ROI-quantified. Worth a 20-min call to see if it'd help your decision?
>
> [Kevin] · [title]

### Email 2 (+3 days) — the proof angle
**Subject:** `re: your NetSuite admin search`
> [First name] — quick add: most NetSuite shops I look at have 5–10 processes quietly eating headcount that AI handles now (AI invoice capture, auto-reconciliation, close checklists, exception handling on integrations).
>
> The audit finds them, quantifies the savings, and tells you what's worth building — guaranteed to identify 3× the fee in annual savings or it's free. 20 minutes?

### Email 3 (+5 days) — the teardown / value
**Subject:** `3 places NetSuite shops burn money`
> [First name], wrote up the 3 most common spots NetSuite mid-market quietly loses money that AI fixes — sharing in case it's useful regardless of whether we talk: [link].
>
> If any of it sounds like [Company], a 20-min call is the fastest way to know what's real. Either way, hope it helps.

### Email 4 (+5 days) — break-up
**Subject:** `closing the loop`
> [First name] — I'll stop here so I'm not cluttering your inbox. If the manual-work-around-NetSuite problem ever gets loud enough to look at, the audit's a low-risk way to get clarity fast. Reach out anytime. Best, [Kevin]

## Rules
- **Personalize line 1** with a real signal/detail. Generic = deleted.
- **One ask** (the call). Never two.
- **Send Tue–Thu, 7–9am or 1–2pm** their time (test).
- **Stop at 4 touches.** Move them to long-term nurture (LinkedIn), don't harass.
- Log every send + reply in the lead CSV.

> Compliance note: keep volume modest and manual early (relationship outreach, not blast). If you scale sending later, follow CAN-SPAM (real address, opt-out). I'll set that up properly when we get there — flagged, not skipped.
