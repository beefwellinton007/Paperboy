## 3. Proof posts (the content engine — 2/week)

Not thought-leadership fluff. Each post = a specific, useful teardown that makes a NetSuite buyer think "he gets my world." Format: hook → specifics → soft CTA.

### Post 1 — the flagship teardown
> **3 places NetSuite shops quietly burn money — and what AI does about it.**
>
> After years inside NetSuite and integrations, the same expensive patterns show up everywhere:
>
> 1. **AP / invoice entry.** Humans keying vendor invoices into NetSuite. AI capture + 3-way match + exception-only review cuts this 70–90%.
> 2. **The monthly close.** Manual reconciliations and a checklist living in someone's head. AI-assisted reconciliation + an automated close checklist takes days off the close.
> 3. **Integration exceptions.** NetSuite ↔ CRM/3PL/bank breaks, someone fixes it by hand at month-end. AI exception handling catches and routes them in real time.
>
> None of this requires risking your system of record — if it's architected right (sandbox-first, controls intact, audit trail preserved).
>
> Most teams don't have a senior person who speaks finance, IT, *and* AI to tell them what's real vs. hype. That's the gap I work in.
>
> Running NetSuite and curious what this looks like for you? DM me "audit."

### Post 2 — the contrarian/honest take
> **Unpopular opinion: don't let an AI vendor near your ERP.**
>
> Most "AI for finance" pitches are built by people who've never seen a SuiteScript governance limit, a sandbox refresh, or a SOX control. They'll happily wire an LLM into your system of record and call it innovation.
>
> AI near your ERP is a *governance* problem first and an AI problem second. The order matters. Here's the safe sequence: [3 bullets — sandbox-first, controls/audit-trail preserved, human-in-the-loop on financial postings].
>
> The right question isn't "can AI do this?" It's "can AI do this without breaking my close or my audit?" Usually yes — if a senior architect designs it. That's the job.

### Post 3 — the ROI/proof angle
> **What a NetSuite AI audit actually finds (a sanitized example).**
> [Walk one anonymized opportunity: process → hours/cost today → AI approach → annual savings → risk note. Concrete numbers. End with soft CTA.]

### Posting cadence
- 2/week (one teardown, one POV/contrarian). Tue + Thu mornings.
- Comment genuinely on target buyers' and partners' posts daily (5 min) — warms DMs.
- Every post ends with a *soft* CTA, never a hard sell.

## Rules
- **Proof, not performance.** If a post wouldn't make a NetSuite controller nod, don't post it.
- **No engagement-bait, no hustle-bro tone.** Senior operator voice. Dry, specific, useful.
- DMs are 1:1 and personalized — never copy-paste blasts (LinkedIn throttles those + they read as spam).
- Track who you DM in the lead CSV.
