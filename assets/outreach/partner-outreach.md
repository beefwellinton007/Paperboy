# Partner-Firm Outreach Scripts

For NetSuite partner firms (Solution Providers, BPO, Alliance, boutique consultancies, solo consultants). **This is your priority channel.** Lead with *their* problem, never your résumé. Goal of first message = a 15-min call, not a sale.

> Replace `[ ]` placeholders. Keep messages short. One specific, genuine detail about *them* beats any amount of polish.

---

## LinkedIn connection request (to firm principal / practice lead)
> Hi [First name] — I work specifically in the NetSuite space on the AI/automation side. Following [Firm]'s work in [industry/region]. Would value connecting with someone building a NetSuite practice.

*(Under 300 chars. No pitch. Just connect.)*

---

I help partners like [Firm] either (a) cover overflow integration/architecture work, or (b) offer an AI capability to your base without building a team for it.

## The 15-min partner call — agenda
1. **Their world (8 min):** "Where's your pipeline tight on senior capacity? What are clients asking about AI? What do you *not* want to build in-house?"
2. **Your fit (4 min):** Position as capacity + ERP-safe AI, *no competition for their clients*. Name the two models (subcontract / white-label).
3. **One small next step (3 min):** "Pick one client or one project — let's do a low-risk pilot so you can see how I work." (One co-sold audit, or one subcontract slice.)

**Do NOT oversell.** Partners buy *safety* and *reliability*. Be the senior, low-drama, client-respecting option. One clean pilot delivery → ongoing relationship → their whole client base becomes your warm pipeline.
