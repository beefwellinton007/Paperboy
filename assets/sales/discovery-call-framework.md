# Discovery / Fit Call Framework (20 min)

The fit call qualifies and sells the **audit** (not the retainer — too soon). Your job: diagnose, quantify their pain in dollars, and make the audit the obvious next step. Talk 30%, listen 70%.

---

## Pre-call (2 min)
- Skim their LinkedIn + company. Note the signal (hiring, go-live, post).
- Have one hypothesis: "I bet their [close / AP / integrations] is manual and expensive."

## Structure

### 1. Frame (1 min)
> "Thanks for the time. Goal of the next 20 minutes is simple: I want to understand how you're running NetSuite today and where the manual pain is, and you get a sense of whether there's real AI/automation value here. If it's a fit, I'll tell you exactly how I'd help. If not, I'll tell you that too. Fair?"

### 2. Diagnose — the 8 questions (12 min)
Ask, then shut up. Quantify everything in **hours and dollars**.

1. **"Walk me through how you're using NetSuite today — what modules, how long live?"** *(context)*
2. **"Where does your team still do manual work *around* NetSuite — spreadsheets, copy-paste, rekeying?"** *(find the pain)*
3. **"Tell me about your monthly close — how many days, what's manual, what's held together by one person?"** *(close is almost always rich)*
4. **"AP and invoice processing — how do invoices get into NetSuite today? How many a month?"** *(quantify: invoices × minutes × loaded rate)*
5. **"What systems are integrated with NetSuite — CRM, 3PL, bank, e-comm? How solid are those integrations? What breaks?"** *(integration surface)*
6. **"Reporting — how much time does your team spend building reports, and do leaders trust them?"** *(reporting pain)*
7. **"What's driving the AI question right now — board, CEO, cost pressure, a specific goal?"** *(find the real urgency + economic buyer)*
8. **"If we took 20–40 hours/month of manual work off your team, what would that be worth — and what would they do instead?"** *(let them size the prize)*

> Throughout: reflect numbers back. "So that's roughly 30 hours a month just on AP — call it $X a year fully loaded. Got it."

### 3. Bridge to the audit (3 min)
> "Here's what I'm hearing: [recap their 2–3 biggest pains, in dollars]. The honest answer is some of that AI can take out today, safely, and some isn't worth it yet — and you shouldn't guess. That's exactly what my NetSuite AI Leverage Audit does: 2 weeks, I map the 5–10 highest-value opportunities across your setup, quantify the ROI, and scope the top few to build. You walk away knowing precisely where AI saves you money and what to do first. It's $4,500 [or $6K], and it's guaranteed to identify at least 3× that in annual savings or you don't pay. Given what you just described, I'm confident we clear that easily. Want me to send over the one-pager and we pick a start date?"

### 4. Close / next step (1 min)
- **Yes/interested:** "Great — I'll send the proposal today; it's a simple e-sign. We can start [date]." → send proposal same day.
- **"Need to think / loop someone in":** "Totally fair. Who else should see the ROI logic? I can join a 15-min call with them." → book the follow-up *now*.
- **Not a fit:** say so honestly, ask for a referral to a NetSuite peer, connect on LinkedIn.

---

## Qualifying scorecard (fill mentally during the call)

| Signal | Good fit | Weak fit |
|--------|----------|----------|
| ERP | NetSuite | Not NetSuite |
| Revenue | $20M–$500M | <$15M |
| Manual pain | Obvious, quantified, painful | Vague, "we're fine" |
| Urgency | Board/CEO/cost pressure | "Just exploring" |
| Authority | Is/knows the economic buyer | No path to budget |
| AI fear | "Won't risk the ERP" (your sweet spot) | Already has internal AI team |

**3+ "good fit" = push for the audit now.** 2 or fewer = nurture, don't chase.

---

## Things to NOT do
- ❌ Don't pitch the retainer yet. The audit earns that conversation.
- ❌ Don't give away the full roadmap on the call — diagnose, don't solve for free.
- ❌ Don't discount when they hesitate. Restate the guarantee instead. ("It's risk-free — 3× or free.")
- ❌ Don't talk tech/AI features. Talk *their* hours and dollars.
- ❌ Don't leave without a defined next step + date.

## After the call
- Send proposal (if fit) **same day** — speed signals seriousness.
- Log outcome + next step + date in the lead CSV.
- If they referred someone, add to list immediately.
