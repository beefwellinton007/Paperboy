# The NetSuite Partner Channel Playbook — Your Cold-Network Cheat Code

This is the highest-leverage section of the whole plan. With a cold network, **borrowing someone else's warm clients is faster than building your own list.** NetSuite's partner ecosystem is purpose-built for this.

---

## Why this works

NetSuite partner firms have three chronic problems you solve:

1. **Capacity gaps.** They win implementation/support deals they can't fully staff with senior people. Junior/offshore handles volume; senior architecture is scarce. They subcontract.
2. **The AI gap.** Their clients are asking "what's our AI plan?" and most partners *have no credible answer*. They can't build ERP-safe AI. You can. You become their AI answer.
3. **Integration debt.** Their clients have brittle NetSuite↔(CRM/3PL/bank/e-comm) integrations. Senior integration architects are rare and expensive.

You show up offering exactly the senior, AI-fluent, integration-capable capacity they lack — and you don't compete with them for the client relationship. That makes you safe to work with.

---

## The two engagement models

### Model 1 — Subcontract (fast cash, time-for-money)
You bill the partner $150–$250/hr for overflow integration/architecture/AI work. They mark it up to their client. You never own the client; you own the relationship with the *partner*.
- **Pro:** immediate, low-trust-required, funds months 1–3, builds case studies.
- **Con:** time-for-money, capped. Use as bridge, not destination.

### Model 2 — White-label AI capability (the scalable one)
You become the partner's "AI Systems Architect" they sell to their entire base. They bring the warm client + the trust; you bring the capability. Delivered under their brand or as their named expert.
- **Audit:** they resell your AI Leverage Audit to their clients (rev-share or flat).
- **Retainer/build:** you deliver, they keep the relationship + a margin.
- **Pro:** scalable, recurring, leverages *their* whole client base — your cold-network problem disappears.
- **Con:** requires a partner who'll commit; takes a few conversations to build trust.

**Strategy: enter via Model 1 (subcontract), earn trust, expand into Model 2 (white-label).**

## Target partner profile (who to approach)

✅ **Best:**
- Small-to-mid **Solution Providers** (Silver / Authorized tier) and **boutique consultancies** — hungry, fast, relationship-driven, no internal AI team.
- **BPO partners** (run clients' accounting on NetSuite) — desperate to automate their *own* delivery margin; the AI-for-finance pitch lands hard.
- **Solo/independent NetSuite consultants** with more demand than capacity — instant subcontract partners and referral sources.

⚠️ **Lower priority:**
- Platinum/Gold giants (RSM, BDO, Sikich, Plative, Deloitte) — have internal teams, slow procurement, hard to reach. Long game only.

## How to find and approach them

### Find
- NetSuite Partner Directory (netsuite.com/portal/partners) and ERP Research's 600+ directory.
- LinkedIn: "NetSuite Solution Provider," "NetSuite Practice Lead/Director," "NetSuite Partner," firm founders.
- NetSuite/SuiteWorld LinkedIn groups, BPO partner lists.

### Approach (sequence)
1. **Warm-up touch:** engage with the firm principal's content / congratulate a partner award / reference a SuiteApp. (Genuine, not spammy.)
2. **Direct message** (see `assets/outreach/partner-outreach.md`): lead with *their* problem (AI gap / overflow), not your résumé.
3. **15-min call:** position as capacity + AI capability, no competition for their clients. Ask about their pipeline gaps and what clients ask re: AI.
4. **Pilot:** propose one small subcontract or one co-sold audit to a client they pick. Low risk for them.
5. **Expand:** after one clean delivery, propose the white-label/rev-share arrangement.

## Partnering terms (keep simple early)

- **Subcontract:** flat hourly ($150–$250), weekly invoice, NDA + simple MSA. No non-compete that blocks your direct business outside their clients.
- **Co-sold audit:** partner takes 15–25% referral, or you flat-rate it and they mark up. Their client, your delivery.
- **White-label retainer:** rev-share (e.g., 70/30 you/them) or partner pays you a wholesale retainer and bills client retail.
- **Protect yourself:** define client-ownership clearly, cap exclusivity, keep the right to build your own direct pipeline.

> Don't over-lawyer the first deal. A one-page MSA + NDA + SOW gets you moving. I've drafted starting points in the proposal template; have a lawyer review before signing anything real.

## The math on why this is priority #1

From the funnel model: partner-sourced leads convert at ~15–25% vs. ~3–5% cold. **One engaged partner with 30 clients ≈ your entire cold-outreach output for a quarter.** Two or three good partners and you may never need to cold-email at scale.

**Ignition-phase allocation: ~50% of outreach hours go here.** It's the difference between a 3-month and a 6-month ramp.
