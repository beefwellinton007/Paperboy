## The core equation

**$20,000/month is ~3 retainers.** That's the whole game. Everything else (audits, builds, subcontracting) either *funds the ramp* or *feeds the retainer funnel*.

```
3 × Fractional AI Systems Architect retainer @ $6,500/mo = $19,500/mo   ← target state
```

Alternative mixes that also clear $20K:

| Mix | Math | Monthly |
|-----|------|---------|
| Pure retainer | 3 × $6,500 | $19,500 |
| Retainer-heavy | 2 × $7,000 + 1 build $8K (amortized over 1 mo) | $22,000 |
| Balanced | 2 × $6,500 + 2 audits × $6K | $25,000 (lumpier) |
| Build-heavy | 1 × $7,000 retainer + 1 build $15K | $22,000 (lumpy, not durable) |

**Durable beats lumpy.** Retainers are the goal because they're predictable and compound. Builds and audits are how you *get* to retainers and smooth cash on the way.

## The funnel math (what volume you actually need)

Work backwards from 3 retainers:

```
Retainers needed (net):        3
Audit→retainer conversion:     ~40%   → need ~7–8 audits delivered to net 3 retainers
Outreach→audit conversion:     ~3–5%  on cold; ~15–25% via warm partner intros
Therefore qualified convos:    ~30–50 to land ~8 audits (cold-heavy)
                               ~12–20 to land ~8 audits (partner-heavy)
```

**Translation:** the partner channel roughly **halves** the conversation volume needed. That's why it's the priority, not an afterthought.

Weekly activity target (Ignition phase):
- 20–30 new cold touches/week (email + LinkedIn), AND
- 3–5 partner-firm conversations in flight, AND
- 1–2 pieces of proof content/week.

This is the real constraint. Capability is not your bottleneck — **conversation volume is.**

## 6-month ramp (realistic, conservative)

Assumes you hold ~25 hrs/week and actually do the outreach. "Bridge" = partner subcontract hours funding the ramp.

| Month | Audits | Retainers (active) | Builds | Bridge subcontract | **Revenue** | Cumulative |
|------:|:------:|:------------------:|:------:|:------------------:|------------:|-----------:|
| 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | $3,000 | **~$9,500** | $9,500 |
| 2 | 2 | 1 | 0 | $3,000 | **~$21,000** | $30,500 |
| 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 ($10K) | $2,000 | **~$30,000** | $60,500 |
| 4 | 1 | 2 | 0 | $1,000 | **~$20,000** | $80,500 |
| 5 | 1 | 2 | 1 ($12K) | $0 | **~$30,500** | $111,000 |
| 6 | 1 | **3** | 0 | $0 | **~$26,000** | $137,000 |

> Notes: Month 2 and beyond look big partly because audits ($6K each) and a build land in-month — **lumpy**. The thing to watch is the **retainer line climbing 0→1→1→2→2→3**. By month 4 you're at a $20K *recurring* base before any lumpy add-ons. Months 1–3 you may dip below this if outreach lags — that's the controllable risk.

**Pessimistic case (outreach is inconsistent):** retainers ramp 0→0→1→1→2→2, run-rate $20K slips to month 7–8. Still a real business.

**Optimistic case (a partner relationship hits in month 1–2):** retainer ramp accelerates, $20K recurring by month 3–4.

## Unit economics per offer

### The Audit (wedge)
- Price: **$4,500–$7,500** (start at $4,500 for first 3 to build proof, then $6,000+).
- Your time: ~8–12 hrs.
- Effective rate: **$375–$750/hr.** Already excellent.
- Strategic value > cash value: it's the on-ramp to everything else.

### The Retainer (engine)
- Price: **$5,000–$8,000/mo**, ~10–15 hrs/mo.
- Effective rate: **$330–$650/hr.**
- 3 of these = the goal. Each is ~3–4 hrs/week of delivery.

### The Build (upside)
- Price: **$8,000–$25,000** fixed-scope.
- Your time: 30–80 hrs depending on scope (may subcontract execution).
- Use to smooth cash and as proof-generators; don't let them crowd out retainer selling.

### Bridge subcontracting (fuel)
- Rate: **$150–$250/hr** through partner firms.
- Purpose: fund months 1–3, build case studies, deepen partner relationships. **Cap it** so it doesn't become a job.

Near-zero to start. Justified spend only:

| Item | Cost | Verdict |
|------|------|---------|
| Domain | ~$12/yr | Yes |
| Email (Google Workspace) | ~$7/mo | Yes |
| Landing page (Carrd/Framer free–cheap) | $0–$19/mo | Yes |
| LLC formation (state-dependent) | $50–$500 one-time | Yes, once first deal is near |
| Scheduling (Cal.com free / Calendly) | $0–$12/mo | Yes |
| LinkedIn Sales Navigator (targeting) | ~$99/mo | **Only when outreach starts** — clear ROI |
| Outreach/automation tooling | $0–$100/mo | Defer until volume justifies |
| **Total to first revenue** | **< $200** | — |

You do **not** need to spend meaningfully before revenue. Sales Navigator is the only spend with obvious ROI early, and only once you're actively prospecting.

## The three levers that move the date

1. **Outreach volume** (biggest). More qualified conversations = faster. This is 80% of the outcome.
2. **Partner relationships** (multiplier). One good partner can replace months of cold outbound.
3. **Conversion craft** (compounding). A sharp audit deliverable + tight discovery = higher audit→retainer rate, which collapses the funnel math.

Price is *not* a primary lever early — don't discount to win; your credibility is the value. Discounting signals you're a freelancer, not an architect.
