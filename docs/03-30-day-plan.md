# 30-Day Execution Plan

**Objective:** End month 1 with the machine built, pipeline live, and **first revenue booked** (an audit verbal/signed or a partner subcontract gig). Not $20K yet — that's months 4–6. Month 1 is about *proof of motion*: assets live, outreach volume sustained, first conversations converting.

**Time budget:** ~25 hrs/week = ~100 hrs for the month. Rough split: **50% outreach/sales, 25% delivery/asset-readiness, 25% foundation/admin.**

> No external action (publish/send/spend) happens without your explicit approval. This plan queues everything for your go.

---

## Week 1 — Foundation + Ignition
**Theme: become sellable and start conversations.** (Full detail: [`docs/04-7-day-sprint.md`](04-7-day-sprint.md).)
- Lock positioning + offer + pricing + guarantee.
- Domain, email, landing page, booking link, LinkedIn rewrite — live by Day 5 (your OK).
- 40-lead list (15 partner / 15 signal / 10 stretch).
- Send 15+ personalized outreaches; 1 proof post.
- **Metric:** 15 touches, ≥2 replies, ≥1 fit call booked.

## Week 2 — Outreach volume + first calls
**Theme: fill the funnel, get on calls.**
- [ ] Send **25 new** personalized touches (partner + signal-based).
- [ ] Round-2 follow-ups to all week-1 non-responders.
- [ ] Run **2–4 fit calls** (20 min) → qualify → pitch the audit.
- [ ] **2–3 partner conversations** in motion (subcontract or co-sell).
- [ ] Post **2 proof items** (a teardown + a short case/POV).
- [ ] Refine messaging from week-1 reply data.
- **Metric:** 40 cumulative touches, 3–5 fit calls done, ≥1 audit proposal out *or* 1 partner pilot discussed.

## Week 3 — Convert to first revenue
**Theme: close the first thing.**
- [ ] Send **25 new** touches (keep top-of-funnel full — don't stall to chase one deal).
- [ ] **Send proposals** to every qualified fit call (`assets/sales/proposal-template.md`).
- [ ] Push for **first audit verbal/signed** *or* **first partner subcontract gig**.
- [ ] If a partner is warm: propose a concrete pilot (one co-sold audit or one subcontract slice).
- [ ] Form **LLC** + set up invoicing (Wave/Stripe) once a deal is verbal.
- [ ] Post **2 proof items**.
- **Metric:** **≥1 paid engagement booked** (audit or subcontract). Pipeline of 3+ live opportunities.

## Week 4 — Deliver + systematize + compound
**Theme: deliver excellence, turn it into the next sale.**
- [ ] **Deliver** the first audit/gig at a high bar (use templates; over-deliver on specificity).
- [ ] On audit readout, **pitch the retainer/build** back-end.
- [ ] Capture a **case study / testimonial** (anonymized if needed) — this compounds every future sale.
- [ ] Send **20 new** touches; book month-2 fit calls.
- [ ] **Retro:** what messaging/channel worked? Double down. Update templates + financial model with real conversion data.
- **Metric:** 1 delivered engagement, ≥1 back-end pitch made, 1 case study captured, month-2 pipeline loaded.

## 30-day scorecard

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Personalized outreaches sent | **85–100** |
| Replies | 10–20 |
| Fit calls completed | 6–10 |
| Proposals sent | 3–6 |
| **Paid engagements booked** | **1–2** |
| Partner conversations active | 3–5 |
| Proof posts published | 6–8 |
| Case studies captured | 1 |
| Revenue (month 1) | **$4.5K–$10K** (audit + possible subcontract) |

If you hit even the low end, you're on the **months-4–6 to $20K** track. The leading indicator that matters most: **outreach volume.** Everything downstream follows it.

---

## What could go wrong (and the fix)

| Risk | Signal | Fix |
|------|--------|-----|
| Outreach stalls (perfectionism) | <15 touches by end of week 2 | Hard quota: 5 sends/day before any asset polish |
| Low reply rate | <5% replies | Tighten subject lines + open with *their* signal, not your résumé; lean harder on partner channel |
| Fit calls don't convert | Calls but no proposals | Sharpen discovery (quantify their pain in $); strengthen the guarantee framing |
| Partner channel slow | No partner pilot by week 3 | Lower the ask: offer one free/cheap subcontract slice to prove value |
| Time crunch from day job | <20 hrs in a week | Protect 2 evenings + 1 weekend block; outreach is non-negotiable, asset-polish is droppable |

---

## Hand-off to month 2
Month 2 = **repeat outreach at volume + deliver + convert audits→retainers.** The retainer line (0→1→2→3 over months 2–6) is the real scoreboard. Keep the funnel full while delivering — the #1 month-2 mistake is going quiet on sales to focus on one delivery.
