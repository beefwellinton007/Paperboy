# Ranked $20K/Month Paths — With Blunt Reasoning

Scoring is against **your** constraints, not a generic operator: 20–30 hrs/week, employed FT (evenings/weekends, free to consult), **cold network**, capital flexible with clear ROI. The cold network is the dominant variable — it penalizes anything that requires a warm list or fast trust, and rewards productized offers, borrowed-audience channels, and credibility-on-paper.

**Scoring key (1–5, higher = better):**
- **Speed** — how fast to first real revenue
- **Ceiling** — can it realistically reach $20K/mo and beyond
- **Fit** — match to your credibility + time + cold-start reality
- **Defensibility** — how hard for others to undercut you

## Why each lands where it does

### 1. ERP-integrated AI automation — productized audit → retainer ✅ PRIMARY
**The move:** Sell a fixed-scope, 2-week **"NetSuite AI Leverage Audit"** ($4.5K–$7.5K) that maps 5–10 AI/automation opportunities across their NetSuite + surrounding systems, ROI-quantified, with the top 1–3 scoped for build. Convert ~40–50% into build projects and/or **Fractional AI Systems Architect retainers**.

**Why it wins for you specifically:**
- **Cold-network-proof.** Strangers won't buy a $6.5K/mo retainer, but a credible senior operator *can* sell a fixed-price, clearly-scoped diagnostic with a guarantee. The audit is the trust-builder that the retainer needs.
- **Your moat is rare.** The ERP × AI overlap is genuinely thin. AI agencies can't pass a NetSuite security/architecture sniff test; NetSuite shops are still bolting on chatbots. You speak CFO, IT, and integration fluently — that's the differentiator.
- **Time-fit.** Audit ≈ 8–12 hrs over 2 weeks, mostly async + 2 calls you schedule around your job. Retainers are 10–15 hrs/month. Three retainers fit inside 20–30 hrs/week with sales headroom.
- **Premium pricing is defensible.** Market data: AI strategy/audit engagements run $5K–$15K; ERP-integrated work commands US-senior rates ($250/hr). You're priced *below* agencies but *above* freelancers, with more credibility than both.

**Blunt risk:** You must produce a genuinely sharp audit deliverable, not a generic "you should use AI" PDF. The template in `assets/sales/audit-deliverable-template.md` is built to force specificity.

### 2. Fractional AI Systems Architect / "AI-enabled CIO" retainers
This is the **back-end** of #1, not a separate path. Standalone, retainers are hard to sell cold (high trust, long sales cycle). Reached *through* the audit, they're a natural upsell. Market: fractional advisory retainers $5K–$15K/mo; premium tiers $10K–$20K. **Three at $6.5K = $19.5K/mo — this is the destination.**

### 3. NetSuite/integration project consulting (subcontract via partners)
**Fastest cash, lowest ceiling.** Partner firms (Solution Providers, Alliance, BPO) constantly need senior integration/architecture hands and will subcontract at $125–$250/hr without you owning the client relationship. Use this as a **bridge** to fund months 1–3 and to build case studies — but it's time-for-money and won't scale to $20K without becoming a job. Treat as fuel, not destination.

### 4. Productized automation assets / SuiteApp / templates (SDN)
Real scalable upside — a packaged NetSuite AI integration (e.g., AI-assisted bank reconciliation, AP automation, AI close-checklist) sold as a SuiteApp or template library. But **slow to revenue** (build + list + distribution) and you'd be selling into the same cold market. Best as **Phase 2**, funded by services revenue, productizing whatever you build twice in audits.

### 5. AI automation for generic non-ERP SMBs ❌ AVOID as primary
Huge supply of $97 "automation agency" operators racing to the bottom. You'd be throwing away your single biggest advantage (ERP credibility) to compete on price in a commodity pool. Defensibility ≈ 1. Hard no as a primary play.

### 6. Cohort course / digital product ("AI for ERP & Ops Leaders")
Genuine **Phase 3** leverage once you have case studies and an audience — turns delivered IP into semi-passive income. But selling info-products cold, with no audience, is slow and adjacent to the influencer game you explicitly want to avoid as a *main* strategy. Park it; build the IP now as a byproduct of delivery.

### 7. Pure hourly NetSuite contracting ❌ TRAP
You can get this tomorrow at $150–$250/hr. But it caps your income at hours-in-a-day, competes with offshore at $30–$100/hr on price-sensitive work, and builds no asset. It's a second job, not a business. Only acceptable as the *bridge* in #3, deliberately and temporarily.

## The synthesis (what we're actually doing)

We don't pick one box — we **stack three**:

```
WEDGE      →  NetSuite AI Leverage Audit ($4.5K–$7.5K, 2 wks)   ← sells cold, fast cash, builds trust
BACK-END   →  Fractional AI Systems Architect retainer ($5K–8K/mo) ← the $20K engine
              + AI Automation Build projects ($8K–$25K)          ← lumpy upside
BRIDGE     →  Partner-channel subcontracting                     ← funds months 1–3, builds proof
PHASE 2    →  Productize a repeated build into a template/SuiteApp ← scalable, later
```

Channel: **NetSuite partner subcontracting (warm-ish) + targeted cold outbound to NetSuite-using mid-market + LinkedIn as proof (not influencing).**
