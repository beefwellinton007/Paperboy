# ICP & Targeting — Who to Go After and How to Find Them

The offer only works if it's pointed at the right list. Two ICPs: **end-buyers** (companies on NetSuite) and **channel partners** (firms that serve them). Work both; the channel is faster.

---

## ICP A — End-Buyer (the company)

### Firmographics
- **ERP:** NetSuite (non-negotiable — it's your wedge).
- **Revenue:** $20M–$500M (sweet spot $30M–$150M — big enough to afford you, small enough to lack an internal AI architect).
- **Headcount:** 50–1,000.
- **Stage/signal:** growing, acquisitive, or recently implemented NetSuite (post-go-live pain is acute).
- **Industries that over-index for ERP-AI pain:** wholesale/distribution, manufacturing, e-commerce/retail, software/SaaS, professional services, healthcare-adjacent, food & bev. (High transaction volume + manual processes = your savings story.)

### Buyer titles (who signs / who champions)
- **Economic buyer:** CFO, VP Finance, COO, CEO (smaller cos).
- **Champion / technical buyer:** Controller, VP/Director of Operations, IT Director, Director of Business Systems, NetSuite Administrator (champions you upward).
- **Influencer:** FP&A lead, Accounting Manager (feels the manual pain daily).

### Strong buying signals (prioritize these)
- **Hiring a NetSuite Admin / ERP Analyst / Business Systems Manager** → they have NetSuite pain and budget *right now*. (Job posts are gold.)
- Recently went live on NetSuite (last 6–18 months).
- Posting about "digital transformation," "automation," "AI initiative," "RPA," "system integration."
- Finance/ops leaders posting about close speed, manual reporting, headcount pressure.
- Recent funding or acquisition (integration + scaling pain).
- Using brittle middleware (Celigo, Boomi, MuleSoft, custom scripts) — implies integration surface area.

### Where to find them (no scraping rules broken — these are public/standard)
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator:** filter by title + company size + "NetSuite" in profile/skills/company tech. (Best single tool — ~$99/mo, clear ROI once prospecting.)
- **Job boards (Indeed/LinkedIn Jobs):** search "NetSuite Administrator," "NetSuite Developer," "Business Systems Analyst NetSuite." Each posting = a warm account.
- **NetSuite user communities:** r/Netsuite, NetSuite Professionals groups, SuiteWorld attendee chatter, NetSuite user LinkedIn groups.
- **BuiltWith / tech-stack lookups:** companies with NetSuite + a CRM/3PL/bank feed = integration surface.
- **Local/industry:** distribution & manufacturing associations, e-commerce meetups (many on NetSuite).

## ICP B — Channel Partner (the multiplier) ⭐ PRIORITIZE

### Who
NetSuite partner firms that need senior AI/integration capacity or a white-label AI offering.

| Partner type | Why they need you | How they pay you |
|--------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Solution Providers** (esp. Silver/Authorized, small/regional) | Win deals they can't fully staff; clients asking about AI | Subcontract hourly; or rev-share white-label |
| **Alliance Partners** | Implementation specialists short on AI/automation skill | Subcontract on projects |
| **BPO Partners** (run clients' accounting on NetSuite) | Want to automate their own delivery + offer AI to clients | Build projects + retainer |
| **SDN / SuiteApp devs** | Need integration architecture or AI feature design | Project / advisory |
| **Boutique NetSuite consultancies / solo admins** | Overflow + no AI capability | Subcontract / partner |

**Best targets = small-to-mid partners**, not RSM/Deloitte. The big platinum firms have internal teams; the **Silver/Authorized and boutique shops** are hungry, fast-moving, and relationship-driven.

### Where to find them
- **NetSuite Partner Directory** (netsuite.com/portal/partners + ERP Research's 600+ partner directory).
- **LinkedIn:** search "NetSuite Solution Provider," "NetSuite Partner," "NetSuite Practice Lead," firm owners/principals.
- **SuiteWorld** ecosystem, NetSuite partner LinkedIn groups.
- BPO partners list (ScaleNorth-type firms) for the accounting-automation angle.

### The partner pitch (one line)
> "I'm a senior AI systems architect who specializes in NetSuite. You've got clients asking about AI you can't fully serve, and overflow integration work you can't always staff. I can be your on-demand AI/integration architect — white-label or subcontract. Want to be the firm that *has* an AI answer?"

## Targeting tiers (where to spend your hours)

| Tier | Target | Effort | Speed to revenue |
|------|--------|--------|------------------|
| **Tier 1** | Small/mid NetSuite partner firms | High | Fastest (warm clients, overflow now) |
| **Tier 2** | Companies hiring NetSuite admins (signal) | Medium | Fast (active pain + budget) |
| **Tier 3** | NetSuite mid-market, no active signal | Medium | Slower (need to create urgency) |
| **Tier 4** | Broad LinkedIn ERP/finance leaders | Low/passive | Long-tail (content + inbound) |

**Allocate Ignition-phase time ~50% Tier 1, 35% Tier 2, 15% Tier 3/4.**

---

## Disqualifiers (don't waste time)
- Not on NetSuite (wrong wedge — unless a partner intro).
- Under ~$15M revenue (can't afford retainers; audits maybe).
- No budget authority + no path to it.
- "Just curious about AI" with no process pain and no champion.
- Wants you hourly, cheap, open-ended (that's the trap, not the business).
