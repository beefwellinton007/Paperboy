# The Offer — Concrete Spec

This is the spine of the business. One wedge offer, two back-end offers. Read the wedge first; it's what you sell cold.

## ⭐ THE WEDGE OFFER

### Name
**The NetSuite AI Leverage Audit™**
*(working name — alternatives: "ERP AI Opportunity Assessment," "NetSuite Automation Teardown")*

### Target buyer
**Primary:** CFO, VP Finance, Controller, VP Operations, or IT Director at a **mid-market company ($20M–$500M revenue) running NetSuite** as their ERP, with 50–1,000 employees.

**Secondary:** Owner/principal at a **NetSuite partner firm** (Solution Provider / Alliance / BPO) who needs to offer "AI" to their clients but can't build it — they buy this as a white-label capability.

**Buyer psychographic:** Knows AI is coming for their function, is under board/CEO pressure to "have an AI plan," is nervous about anything touching the ERP, and has been pitched by generic AI vendors who clearly don't understand financial systems.

### The painful problem (in their words)
- "We're paying six figures a year for NetSuite and it's basically an expensive database — my team still does a ton of manual work in spreadsheets around it."
- "Order-to-cash, AP, and the monthly close are full of copy-paste and reconciliation. People, not systems, are holding it together."
- "Everyone's talking about AI. My CEO/board keeps asking what our plan is. I don't have a credible one, and I'm not letting some chatbot vendor near my system of record."
- "Our NetSuite admin is junior or offshore. Integrations between NetSuite and our other systems are brittle and undocumented."
- "I don't know what's actually worth automating vs. hype. I need someone who speaks finance, IT, *and* AI to tell me the truth."

### The promised outcome
> **In 2 weeks, you'll have a prioritized, ROI-quantified roadmap of the 5–10 highest-value AI and automation opportunities across your NetSuite and connected systems — with the top 1–3 scoped and ready to build — so you know exactly where AI saves real money and headcount, what to do first, and how to do it without putting your ERP at risk.**

Tangible deliverables:
1. **AI Leverage Roadmap** (written) — 5–10 opportunities, each with: process, current cost/time, proposed AI/automation approach, estimated annual savings, effort/risk rating, and dependencies.
2. **ROI model** (spreadsheet) — quantified savings vs. build cost, ranked by payback.
3. **Top-3 build specs** — scoped enough to act on (or to get a clean quote).
4. **Risk & governance notes** — how each touches NetSuite safely (sandbox, controls, audit trail).
5. **60–90 min readout call** — walk the roadmap, answer questions, recommend next step.

### Price point
- **Launch price (first 3 clients): $4,500** — explicitly to build case studies; say so.
- **Standard: $6,000–$7,500.**
- **Guarantee:** *"If you don't find the roadmap worth at least 3× the fee in identified annual savings, you don't pay."* (You'll virtually always clear 3× — manual ERP processes are expensive. The guarantee removes risk and signals confidence.)

### Delivery model
**2-week fixed sprint, ~8–12 hrs of your time, schedulable around your job:**

| Day | Activity | Time | Format |
|-----|----------|------|--------|
| 1 | Kickoff + discovery call | 60–90 min | Call (you schedule) |
| 2–4 | System review (read-only access or guided screen-share) + stakeholder mini-interviews | 3–4 hrs | Async + 1–2 short calls |
| 5–9 | Analysis, opportunity mapping, ROI modeling, write-up | 3–4 hrs | Async (your evenings) |
| 10 | Readout call + deliver roadmap | 60–90 min | Call |

**Why it works:** small check, clear scope, fast, guaranteed, and it gets you inside their systems where the retainer-sized problems reveal themselves.

**Fractional AI Systems Architect** *(retainer)*

### Who
Audit clients who saw the roadmap and want you to drive it — plus run point on AI/automation/integration strategy ongoing.

### Pain solved
"I have the roadmap but no senior person to own execution, govern it, and keep us from making expensive mistakes. I don't need (or can't afford) a full-time AI exec."

### Outcome
A senior architect on retainer who owns the AI/automation roadmap end-to-end: prioritization, vendor/tool selection, build oversight, integration architecture, governance, and translating it for the board — at ~1/5 the cost of a full-time hire.

### Price
**$5,000–$8,000/month**, 10–15 hrs/month, 3-month minimum, monthly thereafter.

### Delivery
Monthly architecture/roadmap cadence + async availability + oversight of builds (executed by you or subcontracted). Schedulable calls; no daytime on-call. **Three of these = your $20K/month.**
