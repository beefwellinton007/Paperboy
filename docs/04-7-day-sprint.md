# 7-Day Launch Sprint — Do This First

Goal of week 1: **go from zero to "able to take money," with the first real conversations in motion.** Not perfect — *live*. Budget ~3–4 focused hours/day (fits 20–30 hrs/week).

> Rule: nothing external goes live or gets sent without your explicit OK. This sprint *prepares and queues* everything; you press the buttons.

---

## Day 1 — Decide & claim the foundation (~3 hrs)
- [ ] **Approve positioning** — read `docs/01-recommended-path.md`, confirm the title ("AI Systems Architect — ERP & Financial Operations") and NetSuite-first focus.
- [ ] **Approve the offer** — read `docs/05-the-offer.md`; lock name, price ($4,500 launch), and the 3×-or-free guarantee.
- [ ] **Pick a business name** + check domain availability (don't buy yet — list 3 options).
- [ ] **Decide entity** — LLC now or sole-prop until first deal (recommend: form LLC once first deal is verbal).
- [ ] **Write your 3 proof points** — sanitized NetSuite/integration/AI wins (drop into `assets/sales/proof-points.md` — I'll create the stub).

**End-of-day state:** positioning + offer locked, name/domain shortlist, proof points drafted.

## Day 2 — Stand up the minimum sellable presence (~4 hrs)
- [ ] **Approve & buy domain + email** (Google Workspace) — *with your OK*.
- [ ] **Finalize landing page copy** (`assets/landing/landing-page-copy.md`) — edit to your voice.
- [ ] **Build landing page** on Carrd/Framer (1 page: headline, pain, offer, proof, CTA → book a call). Keep as draft until you approve publish.
- [ ] **Set up scheduling link** (Cal.com/Calendly) for a "NetSuite AI Fit Call (20 min)."
- [ ] **Draft LinkedIn profile rewrite** (`assets/outreach/linkedin-scripts.md` has the headline + about copy). Don't publish yet.

**End-of-day state:** landing page + booking link ready to go live on your word; LinkedIn rewrite queued.

## Day 3 — Build the lead list (~4 hrs)
- [ ] **Partner list:** 15 small/mid NetSuite partner firms (Tier 1) → `assets/lead-list/lead-list-template.csv`, tag "Partner."
- [ ] **Signal list:** 15 companies currently **hiring a NetSuite admin** (Tier 2) — these have active pain + budget.
- [ ] **Stretch list:** 10 NetSuite mid-market companies (Tier 3).
- [ ] For each: company, contact name, title, LinkedIn URL, signal/why-now, channel.
- [ ] **Consider Sales Navigator** ($99/mo) if manual sourcing is slow — clear ROI once prospecting.

**End-of-day state:** 40-lead list, prioritized.

## Day 4 — Prep all outreach (~3 hrs)
- [ ] Personalize **5 partner messages** (`assets/outreach/partner-outreach.md`) — top 5 partner firms.
- [ ] Personalize **10 cold messages** (`assets/outreach/cold-email-sequences.md` + `linkedin-scripts.md`) — top Tier-2 signals.
- [ ] Load your **discovery framework** (`assets/sales/discovery-call-framework.md`) — know your 8 questions cold.
- [ ] Dry-run the **20-min fit call** out loud once.

**End-of-day state:** 15 personalized messages queued, ready to send on your approval.

## Day 5 — Go live + first send (~3 hrs) — *requires your approval to publish/send*
- [ ] **Publish** landing page + updated LinkedIn (your call).
- [ ] **Send 5 partner outreach** messages.
- [ ] **Send 10 cold** messages (LinkedIn connect + email where available).
- [ ] **Post 1 proof/credibility item** on LinkedIn (a teardown: "3 places NetSuite shops waste money that AI fixes" — `assets/outreach/linkedin-scripts.md` has a draft).
- [ ] Set up a simple tracker (the lead CSV's status column) — who's been contacted, follow-up date.

**End-of-day state:** live, 15 conversations initiated, 1 proof post up.

## Day 6 — Follow-up engine + asset polish (~2 hrs)
- [ ] Set follow-up reminders (48–72 hr cadence) for all 15.
- [ ] Personalize + queue **10 more** leads for Monday.
- [ ] Polish the **audit deliverable template** (`assets/sales/audit-deliverable-template.md`) so you can deliver fast when one lands.
- [ ] Draft the **proposal** (`assets/sales/proposal-template.md`) with your details.

**End-of-day state:** follow-up system running, next batch queued, delivery assets ready.

## Day 7 — Review, respond, plan week 2 (~2 hrs)
- [ ] Reply to every response; book any fit calls.
- [ ] Send round-2 follow-ups to non-responders.
- [ ] Tally metrics: messages sent, replies, calls booked.
- [ ] Set **week-2 targets:** 25 new touches, 3–5 partner convos in flight, 1–2 fit calls → aim first audit verbal by week 3.
- [ ] Log learnings (what messaging got replies) → tighten templates.

**End-of-day state:** week 1 closed, pipeline moving, week 2 loaded.

---

## Week-1 success = these are TRUE
- ✅ Offer + positioning locked
- ✅ Landing page + booking link live
- ✅ 40-lead list built
- ✅ 15+ personalized outreaches sent
- ✅ 1 proof post published
- ✅ Follow-up system running

**Notably NOT required week 1:** a sale. Week 1 builds the *machine*. Revenue is weeks 2–4. If you've done the above, you're exactly on track — keep the outreach volume up.

## The one trap
You'll be tempted to keep polishing assets (comfortable) instead of sending outreach (uncomfortable). **The assets are good enough on Day 5. Send.** Conversation volume is the only thing that produces revenue.
